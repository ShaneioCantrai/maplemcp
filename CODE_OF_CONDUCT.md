# Community Conduct

MapleMCP's public spaces are for testing, documenting, discussing, and improving the product.

Participate in good faith. Critique code behavior, design, evidence, and product decisions rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Security reports belong in the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove material that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project or community.
